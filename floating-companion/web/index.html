Keyboard pilot

Hold W to flap. A/D turn while flapping. R/F pitch up/down while swimming. S stops flapping; it cannot reverse thrust.

Source & assumptions

Inspired by Cuddle-Fish’s two flapping fins and internal weight shifting. Fin thrust uses an estimated membrane model driven by actual joint motion. It is not measured Cuddle-Fish performance.
